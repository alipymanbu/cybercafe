Our Services

At Cyber Cafe, we provide a range of services to meet your needs:

  • Passport Application: Assistance with the application process for passports.
  • PCC (Police Clearance Certificate): Help in obtaining your police clearance certificate.
  • KRA Services: Support with Kenya Revenue Authority services, including tax registration and compliance.
  • NTSA TIMS/DL: Assistance with the National Transport and Safety Authority's Transport Integrated Management Systems and driving license applications.
  • HELB Application: Guidance on applying for the Higher Education Loans Board funding.
  • Australian EOI: Help with the Expression of Interest for Australian immigration.
  • Phone Software Updates: Updating software on mobile devices to ensure optimal performance.
  • Windows/Linux Installation: Installation and setup of Windows or Linux operating systems on your devices.
